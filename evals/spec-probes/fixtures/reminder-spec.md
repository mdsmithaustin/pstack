# Reminder workspace requirements

RR-301. The collections service sends at most two reminders to a borrower in one calendar day. The requirement does not state the timezone that defines a day.

RR-302. The reminder may be sent by SMS or email and may include the borrower's first name and overdue amount. Product wants the message to improve repayment.
