# Fulfillment

The words this codebase uses for getting orders to customers.

## Language

**Shipment**:
The items from one order that leave the warehouse together and travel to one customer address.
_Avoid_: Delivery, package, parcel

**Promised date**:
The date we told the customer their shipment would arrive.
_Avoid_: ETA, due date

**Carrier**:
The company that moves a shipment from the warehouse to the customer.
_Avoid_: Courier, shipper
