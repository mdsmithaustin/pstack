`hermes sessions stats <session-id>` now sums input tokens, output tokens and spend over the session's lineage, from its root through every compression continuation to the tip. A `/branch` copy is its own lineage, so its spend stays separate. The total comes from the new `SessionUsageMixin.lineage_usage`, built on `get_compression_lineage`. `hermes sessions stats` with no id prints the store totals as before.
