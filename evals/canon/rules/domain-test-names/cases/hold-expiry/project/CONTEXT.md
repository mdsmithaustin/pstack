# Box office

## Language

**Hold**
Seats set aside for one customer while they pay. A Hold lasts 15 minutes from the moment it is placed.
_Avoid_: reservation, resv, lock

**Expired Hold**
A Hold whose 15 minutes have passed. Its seats go back on sale.
_Avoid_: stale resv, timed-out reservation

**Booking**
A paid Hold. A Booking never expires.
