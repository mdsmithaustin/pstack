# 11: Short words crowd out the counts

Reported by a user of the word counts.

On our sample, `python3 -m tally sample.txt --top 3` lists "the", "cat", "sat", and on longer files "a" and "on" fill the whole top list. Please add a `--min-length N` option that drops words shorter than N characters, so `python3 -m tally sample.txt --top 3 --min-length 3` still lists "the", "cat", and "sat" but leaves out "a" and "on".
