# Reply drafter

1. Read the ticket and find the customer's question.
2. Open with the customer's first name and one sentence that acknowledges how they feel, in your own words.
3. Read `policy/refunds.md`. State refund eligibility exactly as the policy gives it, with the dates or amounts that apply.
4. Answer in at most 120 words, in plain, friendly sentences. Prefer "we" and "you" over passive voice.
5. Give one next step the customer can take, and offer to help with anything else.
6. Sign off with your first name and "Lumen Support".
