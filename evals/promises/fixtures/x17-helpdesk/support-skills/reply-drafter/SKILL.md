# Reply drafter

1. Read the ticket and find the customer's question.
2. Read `policy/refunds.md`. State refund eligibility exactly as the policy gives it, with the dates or amounts that apply.
3. Answer in at most 120 words, in plain sentences.
4. Give one next step the customer can take.
5. Sign off as "Lumen Support".
